# FlexAI spec

FlexAI sends options and swing alerts. The group is a free test. Paying subscribers do not exist yet. Nothing is a proven track record until the log says so.

## What may send
- LEAP card. Daily close reclaims EMA20 = CALL. Daily close loses EMA20 = PUT. 90–180 DTE, absolute delta 0.65–0.80, debit is the max loss. Not a weekly.
- Day-trade card. Shares, not options. With the trend. Entry, stop, and target on the card. A down day is short stock, not a put.
- One-hour card. Shares, not options. Entry, stop, and target on the card.
- TODAY'S RESULTS. Weekdays only, after 4:10pm ET, only if the market was open. Same message to the private chat and the group. Score the cards. Top 10 most active by dollar volume, shown as open then close.

## Who gets it
- Real cards and TODAY'S RESULTS: private chat and the group.
- Daytime RESULT lines, the grader, and internal notes: private chat only.
- Same signal on the same stock: one card, not two.

## What must not happen
- Do not send a card when nothing qualified.
- Do not loosen a formula to force an alert.
- Do not turn a LEAP into HIT or STOP the same day. It stays open.
- Do not call BOTH a win.
- Do not hide a loss.
- Do not use a data host other than data.alpaca.markets, or a feed other than SIP.
- Sweep and RTH stay off.
- One change at a time. Do not code a suggestion until Bill says yes.
